Adds one line above a server's member list: "1,204 online · 8,930 members".

### Settings

- **Show**: online and total (the default), online only, or total only.
- **Abbreviate large counts**: off by default. Turn it on to read 12.3K instead of 12,345.

The numbers are the ones MaiCord already has for the open server. When they are not known yet the line stays empty, and it fills in as soon as the member list loads. The plugin needs no permissions, sends nothing and stores nothing.

Inspired by Vencord's MemberCount plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
