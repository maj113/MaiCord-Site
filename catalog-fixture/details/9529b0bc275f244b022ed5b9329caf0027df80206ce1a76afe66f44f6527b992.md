When you reply to a message, Discord pings its author by default. This plugin sends your replies without the ping (the reply still shows who it answers).

### Settings

- **Don't ping**: "Everyone except the people below" (the default; with an empty list no reply pings) or "Only the people below".
- **People**: the list the choice refers to. Add someone from the settings page.

It only ever turns the ping off, and only for your outgoing replies: a reply it leaves alone keeps the ping setting you chose in the composer. A rule wins over the ping switch of a reply it applies to.

Inspired by Vencord's NoReplyMention plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
