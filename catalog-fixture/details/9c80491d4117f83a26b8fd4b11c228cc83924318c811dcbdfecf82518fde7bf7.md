Removes the messages of users you blocked from the chat, so you don't see them even when they reply in a busy channel. Nothing is deleted: the messages are only hidden on your screen, and they come back when you turn the plugin off or unblock the user.

### Settings

- **Messages from blocked users**: hide them completely (the default), or fold each one into a single line.

It reads your messages' authors to find blocked users and changes only how they are shown.

Inspired by Vencord's NoBlockedMessages plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
