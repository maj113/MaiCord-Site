Shows a notice when your relationships change behind your back: a friend removes you, someone withdraws a friend request they sent you, you are no longer in a server (removed, or it was deleted), or you are no longer in a group chat.

### Settings

- **Friends who remove you**: on by default.
- **Friend requests that are withdrawn**: on by default.
- **Servers you are no longer in**: on by default.
- **Group chats you are no longer in**: on by default.

Leaving a server or group chat yourself, or removing a friend yourself from MaiCord, is not reported. Changes made on another device can't be told apart from changes made by someone else, so they are reported too.

It only reads friend, server and group chat events and shows notices. It sends nothing and connects to nothing.

Inspired by Vencord's RelationshipNotifier plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
