Blurs the media of messages in age-restricted channels, so they don't show up when someone looks at your screen. Only the images and videos are affected; the text of the message stays as it is.

### Settings

- **Media in age-restricted channels**: blur them (the default) or hide them.
- **Also in DMs and group chats**: off by default.
- **Also on your own messages**: off by default.

It reads which channel a message is in and changes only how its media is shown.

Inspired by Vencord's BlurNSFW plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
