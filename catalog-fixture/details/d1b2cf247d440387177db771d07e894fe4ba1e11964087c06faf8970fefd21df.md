Marks a channel or DM with a small "…" pill, and a server with a dots icon, while someone is typing there. Hover it to see who: "Ada is typing…", "Ada and Lin are typing…" or "Ada, Lin and 2 others are typing…".

The mark goes away ten seconds after the last typing notice, or as soon as that person sends their message.

### Settings

- **Channels and DMs** and **Server icons**: where the mark shows. Both are on by default.
- **Include muted channels and servers**: off by default, so muted places stay quiet.
- **Include blocked users**: off by default.
- **Name who is typing in the tooltip**: on by default. Turn it off to read only "Someone is typing…".

Your own typing never shows. The plugin reads typing notices that MaiCord already receives, keeps the last few in memory only, and sends and stores nothing.

Inspired by Vencord's TypingIndicator plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
