An eye button in the chat bar shows the text in your composer as a message only you can see, in the channel you have open, with a **Preview** card that says it isn't sent. Use it to check Markdown, mentions and line breaks before you send.

### Settings

- **Replace the previous preview**: on by default. When off, every press adds another preview.

It reads your composer only when you press the button, and it sends nothing: the preview exists on your screen only and is gone when you restart MaiCord.

Inspired by Vencord's PreviewMessage plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
