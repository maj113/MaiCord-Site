Adds the group DMs you and someone else are both in to their profile, as chips. Click one to open it. Longer lists end with "+3 more".

### Settings

- **Group DMs to list**: from 1 to 7, five by default. The most recently active come first.

It looks only at the group chats MaiCord already has loaded, so it shows nothing for yourself or for bots. It sends nothing and stores nothing.

Inspired by Vencord's MutualGroupDMs plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
