Double-click a message to act on it without reaching for its toolbar. Double-click **your own** message to edit it; double-click **someone else's** to start a reply. Optionally, delete your own messages with a click while holding a key.

### Settings

- **Double-click your message to edit it**: on by default.
- **Double-click someone else's message to reply**: on by default. The reply is skipped in channels where you can't send.
- **Ping the author when replying**: on by default.
- **Hold a key to double-click**: none by default; choose Shift, Ctrl or Alt to make a plain double-click do nothing, which helps if you often double-click to select words.
- **Delete your messages with a modified click**: off by default. Pick Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Ctrl + Shift. Choose a key that double-clicks don't also need, or the first click of the double-click deletes.
- **Ask before deleting**: on by default; MaiCord shows a confirmation first.

It reads the message you click and starts a reply or edit in the composer, or deletes one of your own messages. System messages, local notices and kept deleted messages are left alone. MaiCord can't see the Backspace key, so the delete click uses a modifier key instead.

Inspired by Vencord's MessageClickActions plugin. Written from scratch for MaiCord; no Vencord code.
